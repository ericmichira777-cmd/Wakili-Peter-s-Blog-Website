About Chuka Legal

Chuka Legal Magazine is an independent legal publication dedicated to providing insightful legal analysis, updates and notes on matters law and justice.
Our goal is to make legal information accessible, informative and relevant to everyone, while promoting legal awareness and critical thinking.
We discuss emerging issues and provide perspectives that contribute to a better understanding of the law for the benefit and success of the students of law.
